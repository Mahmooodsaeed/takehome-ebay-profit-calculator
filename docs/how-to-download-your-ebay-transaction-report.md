# How to download your eBay Transaction report

Takehome needs eBay's **Transaction report**. It's free, and it's the report that includes every fee, ad charge, postage label and refund.

## Steps

1. Sign in to eBay on a computer and open **Seller Hub**.
2. Go to **Payments**, then **Reports**.
3. Choose **Transaction report**.
4. Pick the dates you want: one month, a quarter or the whole year.
5. Click **Create report** (or **Download**). When it's ready, download it as a **CSV** file.
6. Open Takehome and drag the CSV file in.

eBay sometimes moves these menus around. If you can't find it, search Seller Hub for "Transaction report".

## Good to know

- **Use the Transaction report, not the Orders report.** The Orders report has no fees in it, so your profit would be wrong.
- **Use the file exactly as eBay gives it to you.** Don't open and re-save it in Excel first; that can change dates and numbers.
- **Several months?** Add more reports one after another. Takehome combines them.
- **Private:** the file is read on your own computer and is never uploaded.
