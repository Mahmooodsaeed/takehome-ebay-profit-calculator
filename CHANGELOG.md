# Changelog

## Free tools (November 2026)

- New free [eBay fee calculator](https://takehomesellers.com/ebay-fee-calculator) on the website (17 November 2026): every eBay fee for one sale, your payout, profit, margin and break-even price, the price for a target profit, and a link to share a calculation. Covers every eBay.com (US) category and Store level, with fee rates checked on 12 November 2026.
- New free [Takehome connector for Claude](https://takehomesellers.com/claude) (12 November 2026): eBay fee, profit, target price and Store plan calculations inside Claude.

## 1.0 (October 2026)

First release of Takehome for eBay sellers.

- Reads the eBay Transaction report (CSV), one month or a whole year, and combines several reports
- Real profit after eBay fees, Promoted Listings, postage labels, refunds and your own costs
- Order summary with a filter for orders that lost money, and "Got the item back" for returns
- Items view with your best sellers
- Month by month table
- Other eBay charges, like the store subscription, added up by month
- Item costs per item or as one percentage estimate, saved in your browser, with backup to a file
- Other costs: packaging, supplies and postage bought elsewhere
- Print or save as PDF, and export orders as CSV
- Light and dark mode
- Runs offline in the browser: blocked from connecting to the internet
